The Philosophy

You don't need to become an expert before you begin.

Most people spend months trapped in preparation. Watching endless tutorials. Comparing camera gear. Designing logos. Writing ideas for videos they will never make.

You learn by publishing, not by planning. Start small. Put version 1.0 into the world. Learn from the real signal, and make version 2.0 slightly better.

No Guru Hype

I’ve been publishing online since 2009. Here’s what nobody tells you.

I uploaded my first YouTube video in 2009. Over the last 17+ years, I’ve watched algorithms mutate, gear get cheaper, and "gurus" invent 100-step formulas to sell you £1,000 courses.

Here is the truth: 90% of those course frameworks exist solely to justify a high price tag. You don't need a £2,000 setup, a bloated Notion template, or a complex 6-month launch plan. You just need momentum.

01. Low friction beats high production

If your editing or recording setup takes more than a few minutes to prep, you won't publish. Keep it minimal.

02. Done is better than perfect

A published C-minus video beats an unreleased A-plus draft sitting on your computer every single time.

03. Drafts don't count

Creation only counts once it leaves your hard drive. YouTube cannot index an offline draft.

The Framework

Make. Publish. Learn. Iterate.

01

Make.

02

Publish.

03

04

Iterate.

Apply one single 1% improvement to your next piece. Repeat the loop and watch momentum build.
